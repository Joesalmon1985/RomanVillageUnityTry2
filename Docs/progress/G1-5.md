# G1-5 temple and god board view (branch godboard/g1-5-board-view)

Written 19 Nov 2026. Unity view work only; no sim file changed (no sim helper added).

## Done
- **Start:** `-start dev.godboard [-seed n]` or F1 "Starts" (hotkey G) -> `dev.godboard` (`GodBoardStart`, human on seat 0, `GameSetup.GodBoard`). Legacy start unchanged (`LegacyStart_HasNoGodBoard`).
- **Temples (GB-1):** `TempleView`: four temples (one per people) on clear ground beside each first camp (`TempleSites`, uses `CampLayout.Obstruction` + camp props; terrain clearing and walk blocker registered). Primitive art in the people's colour with banner, altar and lamb. Only the player's altar is a `TempleAltar` (IInteractable: tap or E, within 16 m, sends `Sacrifice` and plays the spell light); rival temples are scenery and smoke while that people has the table's turn and the table is not paused.
- **Board view (GB-2, GB-4):** `GodBoardDirector` (installed by `GodBoardInstaller` only on a GodBoard game, from `WorldBootstrap`): camera rises from the player's eye to a top-down view of the island (`RiseSeconds` 2.4), pan by drag, zoom by wheel/pinch/buttons. `GodBoardPieces`: settlement, city and road pieces in each people's colour from `WorldState.Peoples`; gold legal spots (setup corners then road; main phase corners/edges/city upgrades only when affordable); tap picks the nearest legal spot within 24 px and sends `PlaceTableSettlement` / `PlaceTableRoad` / `UpgradeTableCity`. "End turn" sends `EndTurn`; "Return to the world" sends `LeaveTable` and flies back to the altar (walker unlocked, F1 key and pointer restored). `Produced` -> `HexDressingView.Pulse`. Adviser (GB-7): green ring/bar from `TableAi.TargetFor` (main) or best `SiteScore` (setup), toggled by the "Hint" button (`GodBoardDirector.AdviserOn`). A loaded game whose table was left running is paused with `LeaveTable` at scene start.
- **HUD (start of G1-6, `GodBoardHud`, IMGUI scaled by Screen.height/390):** turn ribbon (colour, name, points, AI card counts, current marked), status line, hand with five procedural good icons, hourglass as a shrinking bar, End turn, Return, Hint, Zoom, piece counts, a feed of the last events. `GodBoardHud.TradePanel` (Action<Rect>) and `TradeArea` are the free place for G1-6 (trade panel, offers, costs). No numbers on hexes.
- `Assets/World/Game` only reads state and submits commands.
