# G0-3 join: settlement corners, the war on the god board, the island's fall: progress

Branch `godboard/g0-3-join`, worktree `wt-g03`, cut from `f604837`; `godboard/main` (G2-3 campaign rewiring, `4affb82`) merged in. Sim and EditMode tests only. Written 19 Nov 2026.

## Done

1. **Settlement corners (G0-2 open problem 3).** `TableRules.SettlementRefusal` now refuses (`invalid_site`) any corner where fewer than three hexes meet (`PlotLayout.IsSettlementCorner`, like the world's camps and the ports), in setup and in play. The AI's site search, setup choice, `TableBasicAi`, `TableDefaults` and `TableGrants` all go through this check, so their choices follow without further change. The same check refuses corners closed by a ruin (`TableState.ClosedCorners`).
2. **The war wired into god board games.**
   - `GodBoardModule.InitState` runs `WarModule.InitState` after the plague (its stream forked last, so older streams are unchanged) and syncs the first camps: home camps flagged (WA-15), lodges (WA-1), one garrison soldier each (WA-4). `WorldSimFactory` already calls `GodBoardModule.InitState`, so it needed no change.
   - `GodBoardModule.Register` (new and loaded games alike) registers `WarSystem` (Combat + 10) with a road-link predicate (`GodBoardModule.Linked`: own roads to the lodge, not through another people's settlement; PR-3 stand-in) and `WarTableSync` (Combat + 11).
   - New table seams: `OnBoardChanged` (end of every `AfterChange`: setup builds, builds, losses) → `GodBoardModule.SyncBoard`: every table settlement/city has its war settlement (new ones get their garrison, a city +1, WA-4), war settlements without a table piece are removed, then `WarRules.SetPoints` with the table's points (WA-11). `OnProduced` (once per producing hex and corner) → `WarRules.Deliver` of the same raw goods to that settlement's store (CL-2; cards are unchanged and separate).
   - Burning: `WarTableSync` sees a ruin on a corner the table still holds and calls the new `TableEngine.LoseSettlement(corner, "burned", close: true)`: the corner is emptied, `SettlementLost` and `PointsChanged` are raised at once (TU-6), longest road and the end of the age are rechecked, and the corner is closed until the war clears the ruin (`RuinTicks` 1800), then `TableEngine.ReopenCorner` raises `CornerReopened`. Home camps are only looted (the war module already does this; the table keeps them).
   - G2-3 grants (`TableGrants`, `Built{Free = true}`) call the seamless static `AfterChange`: `WarTableSync` checks `GodBoardModule.WarInSync` each tick and re-syncs (a free city gets +1 soldier, a free settlement its start soldier), and `CatchUpAgeEnded` runs the age-end handling (plague end HI-8, Historic ready) when the table ended without the hook. Both are at most one tick late (the sync runs before the campaign slot 1250).
   - Event order for the campaign (G2-3 CC-7): `War.SettlementBurned` is raised at 1010 and the plague's outbreaks at the table's slot (110), both before the campaign slot 1250, so no `CampaignHooks` call is needed.
   - Soldiers purge demons through the plague (unchanged war module code).
   - Save: `SaveCodec.CurrentVersion` 7. Migration 6 → 7: Legacy gets `War = null`; a god board save gets a fresh war built from its table (settlements, garrisons, home camps, points). New saved fields: `WorldState.War`, `TableState.ClosedCorners`, `TableState.IslandFell`, `WarState.TiedLeadersFight`, `WarState.Stopped`.
3. **The island falls = game over (PL-10, G0-2 open problem 2).** The plague seams (`OnEpidemic`, `OnRoundEnded`) check `Plague.IslandFell` right after the plague step and call the new `TableEngine.IslandFalls`: open offers expire, the table ends for good (Phase `Ended`, `Winner` -1, `IslandFell` true), `GameLost{Cause = "island_fell", Round, Points}` is raised, and the war stops (`WarState.Stopped`; its tick does nothing). The engine no longer starts the next turn or returns to Main after such a seam. `WarTableSync` is a safety net for a fall raised elsewhere. Commands are refused with `table_closed` afterwards.
4. **WA-11 tie rule as a tuning flag.** `WarState.TiedLeadersFight`: `Literal` (two or more peoples sharing the top are at war, so everyone tied at the start is at war) or `OnlyWhenNotEveryoneTied` (tied leaders fight only when someone is below the top). **Default `OnlyWhenNotEveryoneTied`** because the owner has not ruled (question 1 below).
5. **Board proof (start of G1-3):** `GodBoardJoinProof` writes `Docs/progress/G0-3-proof.md` (40 seeds, figures below).

Also: **TR-4 at answer time.** With burnings and grants, points change off-turn, so an offer that was legal when made can reach a people that has since become leading. The proof found one such trade (player-paced variant, seed 3000). `TableEngine.Check(AnswerOffer accept)` now closes an AI seat's offer to an answerer that seat would refuse (`offer_closed`); declining is still allowed.

Reading, honestly:
- The war helps against the plague (95% → 77.5% falls) but the island still falls in most games; PRD wants under 5%. Soldiers are few: garrisons cannot leave (WA-5), so only city second soldiers and lodge-trained soldiers move, and an AI-only round lasts about 150 ticks, so huts (300 to build, 200 a cycle), carrying and training (600) yield about 3.6 soldiers a game.
- With rounds as long as a game with a player, the army grows (8.9 trained, 32 purges) but so does burning (median 6.5 points lost, max 20), and the island falls more often (87.5%); why was not measured (games are longer in rounds, 18 against 15, and the plague draws per round). This variant is the closer picture of a real game.
- War share is high because two peoples tied at the top are at war (WA-11), and ties are common; the literal reading puts 88% of rounds at war.
- Pace is unchanged from G1-2 (median 15-18 rounds against 25-40).
- No AI field was changed and no rule tuned (owner decision). Nothing looked broken in the AI except the TR-4 gap above (an engine check, not a tuning).
