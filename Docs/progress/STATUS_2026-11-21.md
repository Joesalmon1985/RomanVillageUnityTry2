Before this the board tutorial could start on the hub before a place was picked (the player stands within 24 m of the altar) and ring the altar through the vista camera.

The vista lands the player within reach of the altar at the council tower, so the board tutorial began there, stayed active, and shut the council's recorder out (found by the new test: "council tutorial did not begin (active board)").

Owner request: **no roll-number posts on any hex.** `HexLandView` builds the plain marker (halo, stop mark, pick box, no post, disc, number or pips) on every board, the legacy start too; the map screen and the Tab view no longer draw hex numbers; `NumberShown` now means the number disc is visible. The inspect card for a legacy hex still says "A roll of N adds ..." (text, not a post); left as is, say if it should go.
