# G1-6 god board UI: trading, offers, discards (branch godboard/g1-6-board-ui)

Written 19 Nov 2026. Unity view work only; no sim file changed. `Assets/World/Game` only reads state and submits commands.

## Done
- **Trade panel** ("Trade" button under the zoom buttons; `GodBoardHud.Panels.cs`, partial of `GodBoardHud`): tab "Bank or port" (pick a good to give and one to get; each good shows its rate "4 for 1" / 3 / 2 from `TableRules.TradeRate`, the deal is drawn as goods icons, no dice or odds; sends `BankTrade`); tab "Offer to peoples" (To one people or All, +/- counts per good for give and get, sends `OfferTrade`). Refusals are shown as the engine's plain sentence in the panel and the status line. "Wait for your turn." when it is not the player's turn.
- **Incoming offers:** AI offers to the player (or to everyone) show as cards at the bottom right (max 2, "+n more"), with what you get / give as icons, an expiry bar and seconds, Accept (or "Cannot pay") and Decline sending `AnswerOffer`. "Your offer is open: n s" strip for the player's own offer. Feed lines: AI-to-AI `Traded`, offers to the player, answers to the player's offers, `TradeRefused` ("X will not trade with the leader.").
- **Discards (TU-10):** modal picker when `Phase == Discard` and the player owes cards: +/- per good, exactly the owed count, a shrinking time bar with seconds ("then the adviser chooses for us"), "Choose for me" (the AI chooser's pick) and "Give them up" (`DiscardCards`).
- **Epidemic:** on `Epidemic`, red darkening and a "The gods are angry." band for about 3 s, feed line; no number. A notice outside the board view.
- **Game over:** modal end panel from the table state: "The age has turned." with winner and points (`AgeEnded`), or "The island falls." (`GameLost`, `IslandFell`), per-people points, "Return to the world". The rest of the HUD is disabled, `TapAt` returns null (no table input).
- **Esc:** `GodBoardDirector.PressEscape()` closes the trade panel first, else leaves the board like "Return to the world". `Hud.Update` no longer opens the pause menu while the board is up (Rising/Board/Falling).
- Layout in design pixels scaled by Screen.height/390; all buttons 26+ design px; works at 844 x 390 with mouse and touch (IMGUI).
- Fixed on the way: `HexDressingView.Pulse` threw when a scene reload had destroyed the static ring cache (surfaced as soon as tests ran many ticks).
