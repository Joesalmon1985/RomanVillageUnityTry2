# G3-3 soldier, processing hut, lodge and burning views (branch godboard/g3-3-war-view)

Written 19 Nov 2026. Unity view work only; no sim file changed. `Assets/World/Game` only reads state and events.

## Done
- `Assets/World/Game/War/` (installed by `GodBoardInstaller` after the plague views, when `WorldState.War` exists):
  - **Soldiers** (`SoldierView`, `SoldierMeshes`, `WarViews`): one view per soldier at its corner or plague node (ring around the spot), walking straight between node and corner along the trail (position from `MoveLeft`, start from `SoldierStepped`). People colour from `WorldState.Peoples`. Five looks (Brute maul and bulk, Raider axe and red paint, Shieldbearer spear and large shield, Skirmisher javelins and feathers, Warrior spear, crest and buckler) from box/cone primitives. Gear level shows: weapon head size, shield size and rims, clay plates, paint stripes, feathers, mantle, antlers.
  - **Duels and wear (WA-7..WA-9):** the pair faces off beside the node/corner; alternating swings from `DuelSwing`; target plays dodge on a miss, block (fresh, shielded) or hit stagger, delayed to mid-swing. Wear needs no bar: stagger on hits (harder when hurt), limp when Hurt, heavy limp plus a dropped shield (falls and lies, then goes) plus a slower swing and a sagging weapon when BadlyHurt. Death: fall, lie, sink away; killer cheers. Peaceful peoples pass because the sim never pairs them.
  - **Purging:** a soldier with `PurgeLeft > 0` plays a repeating overhead slam facing the node; `DemonPurged` plays one at once. The effect itself is G2-2's `Banished` effect (already fires for soldiers).
  - **Huts, lodge, piles** (`WarSites`): one hut model per `Hut` (scaffold rises while building, a prop for each of the ten goods, chimney smoke while both inputs are in store, pile of its processed goods, a pop on `GoodProcessed`). A war lodge (gabled house, banner, weapon rack, training post, palisade, fire pit while training, queue crates) at each lodge corner, with ring, flag wave on `SoldierTrained` and the new soldier cheering. Non-home settlements with no other view get simple ground dressing; home camps without one get tents.
  - **Burning and looting:** `SettlementBurned` (or a ruin in state with no settlement): buildings sink and lean, fire, smoke, then a ruin model; it stays until the ruin leaves state (`RuinCleared`/`CornerReopened`), then the site goes. `CampLooted`: scattered baskets, scorch, torn banner, thin smoke for 150 s.
  - **God board:** every soldier shows a flat marker (people colour, look pip) on the board and God camera.
- **AB-1 fix (asked by the coordinator):** the "5" with pips on the plague nodes was the legacy hex number token (`HexLandView`, `Token`, post, disc, pips) of the hex whose centre sits at a node, plus the "+n" paid count. Both are now hidden in GodBoard games (`HexLandView.HideNumbers`, set in `LandViews` from the ruleset; legacy games unchanged). Files: `View/Land/HexLandView.cs`, `View/Land/LandViews.cs`. Test: `NoNumberTokensInAGodBoardGame`.
- Capture and probe drivers: `AgentScripts/WarDrive.cs` (classes `WarDrive`, `WarDrive2`; writes soldiers, wars and ruins into state for captures only; free `CapCam`), `AgentScripts/WarProbe.cs`.
