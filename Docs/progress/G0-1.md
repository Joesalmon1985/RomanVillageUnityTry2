**The board deal (BD-1 to BD-6)** in `Sim/Board/BoardDeal.cs`, `BoardGenerator.CreateDealtBoard(seed)`:

- Meadow layout of the legacy table; the seven pinned hexes; 31 hexes dealt from 6 Forest, 8 Pasture, 7 Fields, 5 Hills, 5 Mountain; the 38 numbers of BD-3 reshuffled until no 6/8 touches another 6/8.
- Starts fixed at 70, 97, 57, 82 (`Board.StartCorners` length 4). Up to `DealTries = 500` tries with sub-seeds `deal.t`; the first fair try (every start on three different raw terrains, summed pips max - min <= 1) wins, else the fairest (fewest bad starts, then smallest spread). **Decision:** once a try finds a terrain deal that gives every start three different terrains, later tries keep that terrain and only redeal the numbers. With a full redeal every try only 97.7% of 1000 seeds were fair; with this 1000/1000 are (average 24 tries, at most 112). `Board.DealTriesUsed`, `Board.DealFair` record it.
- Ports: nine fixed spots on Meadow rim hexes (0,4), (-1,4), (-3,4), (-4,4), (-4,2), (-4,0), (3,-4), (4,-4), (4,-2); kinds (four 3:1, five 2:1 one per raw good) shuffled from `board.ports`. The rim has only eleven allowed hexes: (4,0) and (-1,-3) hold tower corners 74 and 17, and the rest of the east and south shores are campaign places or resource hexes, so the largest gap round the shore is 106 degrees. `Board.Ports` (`Port`: hex, its settlement corners, ratio, good). Who may use a port is for G1-1.
- Suggested settlement corners keep clear of all four starts; the dance circle plot is reserved as before.
- `CreateMvpBoard` is unchanged for the legacy ruleset (its site-suggestion and dance-circle code was only moved into shared helpers).
