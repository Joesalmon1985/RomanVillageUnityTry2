## Seams for G0-2's wiring

- `CampaignRuleset.GodBoard(WorldState)`: the switch (now `s.Table != null`).
- The campaign slot (1250) reads this tick's `War.SettlementBurned`, `Plague.Outbreak` and spread `Plague.Infected` events by itself. The table (110) and war (1010) run before it, so if the plague is driven from the table no call is needed. If something raises them after 1250, call `CampaignHooks.OnSettlementBurned(s, people, corner, ev)` / `CampaignHooks.OnOutbreak(s, node, ev)` (safe to call twice in a tick).
- `CampaignHooks.HomeNodes(s)`: plague nodes on the home camp's hexes.
- `CampaignHooks.CrystalGained(s, element, people, restored, ev)`: cure/strengthen + grant + spire (called by the campaign itself).
- `TableGrants.GrantCityOrSettlement / GrantCity / GrantSettlement / BestCityCorner / BestSettlementCorner`. **The war module is not told** about a granted city or settlement: whoever wires `WarRules.AddSettlement` / `UpgradeCity` to table builds should also react to `Built{Free = true}`.
- Save: `CampaignState.Spires`, `CampaignState.LastHomeTroubleTick` (plain fields; the campaign is already saved whole).

## Race on the god board (CC-2, PRD_GODBOARD 12.2)

`CampaignGodBoardRaceTests`: four peoples on a dealt board with table and plague (not opened, so no epidemics or burnings), campaign started in the first minute, 11 seeds, written to the test output only.

- Whole campaign (race-sized skeleton, authored march times): **direct** player first on 2 to 4 crystals in every seed (four seeds 2, one 3, six 4), 22 to 36 min; **reluctant** ("Not that way" to every untaken crystal) first on 0 in every seed. Both asserted.
- Earth slice (authored): direct first 11 of 11 (restored 5:18), reluctant stolen 11 of 11. Asserted.
- **Board** player (at the god board, back one minute after Mira starts to wait, prompt while there): first on 0 to 2 per campaign (median 1), Earth stolen 11 of 11. Measured only, not asserted.
- Without the legacy overruns the Ash expedition is never paused on the god board; the targets hold anyway, so march times are unchanged. Trouble at home was not in these runs (war and plague not driven); with them the Ash clock gets +900 per trouble, which can only make the race harder for the player.
