# G2-2 plague views (branch godboard/g2-2-plague-view)

Written 19 Nov 2026. Unity view work only; no sim file changed. `Assets/World/Game` only reads state and sends `Banish{Node}`.

## Done
- `Assets/World/Game/Plague/PlagueViews.cs` (installed by `GodBoardInstaller` after the director, only when `WorldState.Plague` exists). Positions come from `PlagueGraphData`; demon counts from `PlagueState.KindCount` every frame.
  - **Ground:** a cairn shrine with an element-coloured flag at each of the 48 nodes (tap = banish if near), faint trail paths (tunnel not drawn on the ground), demon figures by kind (0-3, element-tinted horned figures), scorch ring and a spire while demons stand there. Terrain props are cleared around nodes (`LandViews.AddClearing`), but trees built earlier may still stand near a shrine.
  - **Overlay (PL-12):** key V or the "Plague" button (`PlagueHud`). On the board / God camera an elevated layer (y 80): four regions tinted by element (Earth brown, Fire red, Water blue, Air violet), 73 trails (crossings gold with a diamond, tunnel dashed violet with a diamond), 48 node discs with a three-segment demon ring coloured by kind (red outer ring at 3 = next demon outbreaks). On foot: bright trails and a beam over every node. Panel: outbreak pips of 8, rate track, top of the discard pile (element, node), region legend.
  - **Outbreak chains:** a glowing pulse runs along each trail from the outbreaking node, delayed by chain step; flash on the source.
  - **Purge:** `Banished` event (player or soldiers) plays a ring, pillar and sparks in the demon's colour.
  - **Epidemic (PL-6):** on `Plague.Epidemic` (and island fall): ~9 s storm: sun, moon, ambient and fog dimmed, board background darkened, violet screen tint, two lightning flashes, procedural thunder. No numbers. Feed line now "The gods are angry." (director, one string).
  - **Banish on foot (PL-11):** key B, tap on the shrine, or the "Banish [B]" button (shown near a node with demons, and during the cooldown as a bar with seconds). Range 18 m; sends `Banish{Node, Kind=-1}`, plays the gold spell effect. Refusals are said in a bubble.
- Small edits outside the new folder: `GodBoardHud.ExtraOver` (static hook, so presses on the plague controls are not board presses), director install line and the feed string.
- `AgentScripts/PlagueDrive.cs`: play-mode driver for captures (`Mix` writes demons straight into state for the capture only).
