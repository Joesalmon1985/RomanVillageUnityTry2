# WU-1b: the hub's input and return view

20 Nov 2026, branch `godboard/wu1b-hub-input` (from `5236316`, the WU-6 merge). Second half of WU-1 (WORLD_UI_PLAN 2.1, P-3, P-7). No sim file changed; `Assets/World/Game` only reads state and sends the same commands the conversations already sent. `Board/GodBoard/*` and `Council/*` are untouched.

## Done

1. **Tap the ground to walk** (`Player/GroundWalk.cs`, one branch in `PointerInput.Tap`, new `TapOutcome.Walk`). A tap that hits plain ground or nothing (and is not a castable ring, a person, a prop or an action) walks to the nearest free lattice cell (`WalkGrid.NearestFree`, `WalkGrid.FindPath`, the Tab map's walk) and a footprint ring marker (two footprints, warm glow) lies on the target cell until arrival (fades) or a stop (gone). Not in God View, underground (the footprint stones and keys keep that) or with a locked walker. Range 160 m. People, props and `IWorldAction`s keep the old priority (`IWorldPrimary` untouched); the hex-inspect bubble of a ground tap is replaced by the walk (it still shows if the walk is refused).
2. **Slabs do not swallow ground taps.** The temple's pick box was a 9.5 x 6 m slab over the whole forecourt; it is now only the doorway step (3.6 x 1.8 m). The ring's box is the ring itself (a tap inside the fence previews the duel, nobody can walk in there anyway); the lodge box is the building. The new entry stones are about 1.5 m boxes.
3. **The large sand slab in the spawn view** (bottom left of `hub_spawn_844.png`) was a **plague trail strip** (`PlagueViews.BuildGroundTrails`, sand strips 1.8 to 2.6 m wide, y 0.14, unlit) that runs a few metres from the spawn cell and reads as a flat slab at eye height; the brown path in the middle of the view is another one. Segments are now cut to the part outside the hub clearing (`CampLayout.HubRadius`, only when the board is a dealt hub); the plague overlay and the lodge map table still draw every trail. The green clearing now reads as green from the spawn (captures).
4. **Drag sideways on empty scene turns one sixth per drag step** (`DragLook` in `GroundWalk.cs`, `PointerController`). A press that starts on empty ground or sky (not on a person, prop, action, bubble, ledger card or the touch band) and moves more than 12 px is a drag; step = max(40 px, 12 % of the screen height); finger right = view turns left (the world follows the finger). Turns queue and the walker takes them one at a time. A press is now a tap **on release** (a drag never taps; a press in the strip band never taps the world, so the mouse on a strip no longer also walks). Keys and strips unchanged.
5. **Two-tap hub entries** (`View/Camp/HubEntry.cs`, `HubEntryAction`: preview with the `LedgerCard` line and a warm ground ring, second tap commits the same call as the conversation; the conversations stay as the long form):
   - lamb pen by the temple: "Take a lamb to the altar?" -> `TempleForecourt.BeginOffering` (refused with the altar's own range line when far);
   - the tower's bench beside the steward: "Hear the dispute?" -> `CouncilDirector.StewardOpen` (disabled with the honest line when no dispute is called);
   - the practice ring (`RingTap` is now an `IWorldAction`): "Fight a practice duel with Mira?" -> `DuelRing.StartDuel`, preview ring round the fence;
   - Sol's seat: "Dance at the fire?" (circle warm) or "Follow Sol to the spires?" (cold) -> the rhythm party / `SolGuide.LeadToSpire`.
   Key (E) reaches: ring 7 m, bench and Sol's seat 4 m, pen 0 (E near the temple keeps picking the altar).
6. **Return to the hub view.** The god board, council, dance circle, practice duel and lodge inspection already come back to the same cell, heading and walking view (the encounter host restores the pose; the board never moves the walker); no code was needed, a test now proves it for each (see tests). **Dungeon:** "Lead me out" ends at the glade stair mouth, the same cell the player left from (existing `ExpeditionTests.LeadMeOut_FromACompanionCard...`). A dealt god-board hub has no expedition or stair yet (`the stair link is null there; Mira's expedition on the god board is a later package), so "back at the hub pose after the dungeon" could not be run on a real hub; see open problems.
7. **Framing on four seeds.** Measured seeds 7, 3000, 3001, 42 at the spawn: identical (the hub layout depends on the island, not the deal): temple 4 deg, tower 14, circle -43, Mira 38 (all in the first view), lodge -112 and ring +111 (one turn away). No change to the spawn-heading rule was needed; a test covers all four seeds.
8. **Side item: `DanceTests.MidSongPause_FreezesTime_ResumesAfterFourCount_ScoringExact`.** It passed here on the first run (15 / 15), so it is load-dependent, but the code really did let song time move: `StemPlayer.SongTime` froze only while paused and not resuming; during the four-count it returned `smoothed DSP - StartDsp`, and `SmoothedDsp` extrapolates up to 100 ms ahead of the raw DSP clock, so in the last frames of the count (raw clock still short of `ResumeAtDsp`, `TryFinishResume` not yet true) the song time read the paused time plus 1 to 4 ms, against the test's 1e-9 tolerance. The test is right (the spec says song time holds during the count). Fix (one line): `if (IsPaused) return PausedSongTime;` for the whole pause including the count. Scoring is untouched (the session is not advanced while paused; after the count song time continues from the same value; the audio restarts on the same DSP time). `DanceTests` 15 / 15 afterwards.
