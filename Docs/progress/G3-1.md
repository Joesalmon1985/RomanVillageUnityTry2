- Events: HutBuilt, GoodProcessed, GoodLodged, TrainingStarted, SoldierTrained, SoldierStepped, DuelStarted, DuelSwing, DuelEnded, SoldierDied, DemonPurged, SettlementBurned (PointsLost 1 or 2), CampLooted, RuinCleared, WarStateChanged.

## Rule decisions (check against the owner's intent)
- WA-11 taken literally: if two or more peoples share the top score they are at war, so a fresh game where everyone is tied is at war. The turn engine may want to call `SetPoints` only once points exist, or the owner may want "tied at the top and 2+ above the rest".
- War is mutual: when A leads by 2 the leader's army also treats the others as enemies (it may attack them).
- A 3-demon node cannot be passed, but a soldier may step onto one as the last step of a purge order, and may leave one it stands on.
- Priority 2 (WA-13) applies to every army including the leader's (nodes whose hex touches a non-leading people's settlement). Leader = unique top scorer. Demons on a 3-demon node across the route become a priority-1 purge target (blocker fallback); not directly tested.
- Home camps with an empty store are not attacked (nothing to loot). After looting the attacker withdraws to a node.
- Idle soldiers at a node walk back to the nearest own settlement. No healing exists (the PRD has none).
- Burning moves every soldier at that corner onto a node; the caller applies `PointsLost` (TU-6).
- Duel pairing is by standing at the same node or corner; `EngageRadius` 6 m is a view concern.
