# G1-7 collecting villagers and god-board polish (branch godboard/g1-7-villagers)

Written 19 Nov 2026. Unity view work only; no sim file changed. `Assets/World/Game` only reads state and sends commands.

## Done
- **Collecting villagers (CL-1, CL-2)** `Board/GodBoard/CollectWalkers.cs`, created by `GodBoardDirector` (`Collectors`) and fed from the `Produced` event. For every producing hex, each owned corner sends one villager per card (settlement 1, city 2), at most `CapPerSettlement` = 3 per settlement per event. The villager walks corner to hex centre, gathers for 0.6 s, and walks back with a load box in the good's colour; staggered by 0.45 s. Placeholder figures drawn 14x life size with a bright cap so they read from the whole-island view (a smaller figure was invisible at the board camera's height of about 1400 m). Max 90 alive. Visual only: the cards and the stores' goods come from the sim (G0-3 `OnProduced`), so CL-2 needs no view work. Test hooks: `Sent`, `Delivered`, `Active`, `Step(dt)`.
- **Prince guide (GB-7)** `Prince/PrinceDirector.GodBoard.cs`: in a GodBoard game he comes to the player, greets (replies "Lead the way" / "Not now", or leads by himself after 14 s), leads them to Mira (player auto-follows, he waits and calls if the player lags), says who Mira is, then points at the temple and tells them to sacrifice a lamb. No first scene, no pitches, no announcements in GodBoard games. A game already at the board skips the welcome. Guide state (`Guide`) is not saved. Adviser: when the board's hint is shown on a new spot, the prince says one line in his bubble and the board feed shows "The prince: ..." (at most one per 8 s).
- **Mira from minute one (CC-1)** `Board/GodBoard/MiraOffer.cs`, wired in `CampInstaller.SpawnMira` for GodBoard games: her dialogue offers "Set out for the crystals" (starts the campaign through `CampaignStart.StartNow`), Practice duel, Maybe later. `PrinceDirector.After.MayStartCampaign` no longer needs Skirmish mode on GodBoard (legacy unchanged).
- **AB-1** no roll text: the first-person HUD strip (`Hud.StripLines`) shows only the time on GodBoard; the Tab map's hex number discs, the hex number tokens in the world (`HexLandView`, dealt boards) and the god-view labels (`TerrainBuilder`, dealt boards) are gone. F1 tools untouched.
- **HUD status** `GodBoardHud.StatusLine`: no more "People -1 play."; before the board opens it says "Sacrifice a lamb at your altar to open the board.", else "The peoples are gathering.".
