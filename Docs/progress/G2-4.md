# G2-4 crystal spires and the rhythm game at them (branch godboard/g2-4-spires)

Written 19 Nov 2026. Unity view work; no sim file changed.

## Done
- **Spires (CC-5):** `SpireDirector` (`Assets/World/Game/Spires/SpireDirector.cs`, installed by `GodBoardInstaller`) builds a spire on `SpireRaised` and from `CampaignState.Spires` at scene start (so a load restores them). Original primitives: stone plinth, tapering shaft in the element colour (Earth brown, Fire red, Water blue, Air violet), floating crystal, buttresses, additive light beam and a point light. Spires are separate objects, so they stay when the settlement burns. Blocked for walking.
- **Stage (SP-1, SP-4):** each spire has a stage 16 m or more from its corner on clear ground (avoids plots, roads, temples, other stages): a dais with a `DanceCircle` (fire, four drums, dancers), spire behind the fire. Tap the spire or stage (or E) within 22 m (`SpireStage.Interact`) starts `rhythm_party` with `RhythmRequest`. Any spire, rivals' too. The home dance circle is retired (hidden, blockers removed) when the first spire rises; Sol's "Dance at the fire" then plays at spire 1. Sol stays home; his lines show as bubbles in front of the player.
- **Per-spire packs (SP-2, SP-3):** spire 1 Lv1 Warehouse (as before); 2 `lv2_alien_spaceship`; 3 `gospel_temple`; 4 `gospel_storm`, by order of rising. `LocalRhythmPacks` reads pack.json and stem WAVs at run time from a local folder; any failure falls back to spire 1's chart and track (`PackFellBack`). Nothing from the packs is committed.
- Small changes: `CampLayout.ForStage`, `DanceCircle.Build(.., makeCurrent)`, `Retire()`, `RhythmPartyEncounter` reads `RhythmRequest` (new `SpireIndex`, `PackId`, `PackFellBack`; header shows spire and BPM).
- Dev: `AgentScripts/SpireDrive.cs` (`Begin`, `Day`, `Raise n`, `Approach i m`, `ToSpire i`, `Dance i auto`, `Info`, `Shot`). `Raise` builds views only (no sim change).
