A Slow or Absent player lets the army go unused and gets burned (median 3 and 6 points lost).

## Options for the owner (AI seat, 48 seeds, rules otherwise unchanged)

Targets: island falls under about 10%, rounds to win 25-40, war under about 30% of rounds. Plague changes are the only thing that stops the falls; the tie rule is the only thing that fixes the war share.

| Option set (smallest first) | Island falls | War share | Rounds to win, median (range) | Won 25-40 | Quick wins | Absent wins |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Default | 68.8% | 54.5% | 16 | 0 of 15 | 12.5% | 0% |
| **A.** Infect every 3rd round + tied leaders never fight (2 changes) | **0.0%** | **15.9%** | 18 (11-26) | 1 of 48 | 37.5% | 0% (Absent falls 6.3%) |
| **B.** A + win at 13 points (3 changes) | **6.3%** | **22.0%** | 23 (16-30) | 15 of 45 | 31.3% | 0% (Absent falls 20.8%) |
| **C.** B + war lead 3 (4 changes) | **4.2%** | **11.1%** | 22 (15-29) | 8 of 46 | 31.3% | 0% |
| **D.** Infect every 2nd round + grace 10 + win 13 + ties never fight + war lead 3 (5 changes) | **2.1%** | **11.0%** | 22 (16-30) | 13 of 47 | 35.4% | 0% (Absent falls 6.3%) |
| Infect every 3rd + win 14 + ties never + lead 3 | 8.3% | 13.2% | 23 (16-29) | 16 of 44 | 27.1% | - |

(Columns for Quick and Absent are from the finalists table of the study; options A to D are the study's F1, F3, D5 and D8.)

What did **not** help, measured singly: grace 6 to 10 (68.8% to 45.8%), rate track 1,1,1,2,2,2,3,3,4 (no change), rate advancing every 2nd or 3rd epidemic (no change), island falls at 10 outbreaks (no change), epidemic placing 2 demons (56%), two soldiers per settlement (87.5% falls, more burning), win at 12 or 13 alone (falls rise to 88% to 94% because games are longer), WA-11 literal (war 86%), war lead 3 or 4 alone (war 47% to 49%).

**Rounds to win 25-40 is not reached by any set** (best median 23 to 26, and 26 only where 40% of games end in the island's fall). The pace is set by how fast the AI builds, not by the plague. Win points 14 gives a median 23 to 26 but only while the plague is slowed; 15 points makes falls return (48%). To reach 25-40 something else has to slow building (production per roll, costs, pieces, or the number of turns per round); that is outside this study's knobs and is the owner's decision.
