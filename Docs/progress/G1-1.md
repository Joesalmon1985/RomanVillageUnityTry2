# G1-1 turn engine: progress note

Branch `godboard/g1-1-table`, worktree `wt-g11`. Written 19 Nov 2026 at the end of the session.

## What is done

A new pure-sim module `Assets/World/Sim/Table/` (namespace `World.Sim.Table`) with state `WorldState.Table`:

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `TableState.cs` | `WorldState.Table` (`TableState`), `TablePeople`, `TablePort`, `TradeOffer`, `TableTuning` (every number of PRD 4.4 to 4.6 as a field) |
| `TableCommands.cs` | Commands, events and refusals of Appendix A |
| `TableRules.cs` | Read-only rules: costs, placement, piece counts, port rates, points, longest road, pips |
| `TableEngine.cs` | Checks and applies commands; the per-tick system; `TableSeams` (hooks); `TableActor` (AI seats act through the same checks); `TableDefaults` (default discard and setup choosers) |
| `TableModule.cs` | `InitState(s, peopleCount, startCorners, humanSeat = 0, ports = null)` and `Register(sim, seams = null)`, which returns the seams |
| `TableBasicAi.cs` | Stand-in AI seat: builds whenever affordable (city, settlement, road towards a spot) and trades surplus 4:1 with the bank. No offers. G1-2 replaces it. |

Rules covered (all for any N; nothing uses `PlayerIds.Count`):

- **GB-5, GB-6:** nothing happens before `Sacrifice`; it opens the setup round. Each people already has its first camp (a settlement, 1 point) at its start corner. In seat order each places a second settlement (distance rule, any corner, no road needed) and a road touching it, and gets one card per resource hex beside that settlement. AI seats place after `AiSetupTicks` with `TableSeams.AiSetup` (default: most pips, then most kinds, then lowest id). The player is not timed in setup.
- **TU-1:** turn order shuffled by seed after setup (`TableState.RollRng`, forked from the world RNG as `"table.roll"`); a turn = hidden 2d6, production, actions, `EndTurn`. Rounds count from 1.
- **TU-2:** settlement 1 card, city 2, Meadow nothing; a hex is skipped when `TableSeams.HexBlocked(s, hex)` is true (default never; the plague wires it). `Produced` lists the hexes, the blocked hexes and the gains, never the roll. The roll is kept in `TableState.LastRoll` for F1 and dev.
- **TU-3 to TU-6:** costs, distance rule, road connection (roads do not run through another people's settlement), piece limits 15/5/4 (a city gives its settlement piece back), longest road (at least 5, ties keep the holder, a tie without the holder means nobody holds it), points computed from the board.
- **TU-7:** `AgeEnded{Winner, Points}` when a people reaches `WinPoints` = 10 at the end of the action; the table then closes (`table_closed`) and nothing moves.
- **TU-8:** no development cards, no robber.
- **TU-9:** hands are counts per raw good, separate from physical goods.
- **TU-10:** on a 7, every people holding more than 7 cards owes half (rounded down). AI seats discard at once with `TableSeams.ChooseDiscard` (default: largest pile first); the player has `DiscardTicks` = 150 to send `DiscardCards`, then the default chooser picks. The turn waits; then `Epidemic` is raised (no production on a 7). The wait does not use up an AI's turn time.
- **HG-1 to HG-6:** the player's sand: 450 at turn start, cap 900, 1× for 40 ticks after an action then 3×, +50 per accepted action (build, trade, offer, answer), `EndTurn` ends the turn, empty sand ends it (`TurnEnded.Reason` "sand"). AI seats are asked once per turn (`TableSeams.AiTurn`) and their turn lasts `AiTurnTicks` + `AiActionTicks` per accepted action.
- **GB-4, HG-6:** `LeaveTable` pauses the whole table (sand, turns, AI seats, offers, the discard window); `Sacrifice` resumes it. Commands are refused with `table_closed` while paused.
- **TR-1:** bank 4:1; 3:1 or 2:1 when the people holds a settlement or city on either corner of a port. The board has no port data yet, so ports are passed to `InitState` (none = bank only).
- **TR-2, TR-3 (state only):** `OfferTrade` to one people or everyone on your turn; `AnswerOffer` accept or decline; AI seats answer after `AiAnswerTicks` = 20 through `TableSeams.AiAnswer` (default: decline); an offer to everyone goes to the first acceptor in id order, and closes when all have declined; offers expire after `OfferTicks` = 150 or at the end of the turn. AI seats can make offers through `TableActor.OfferTrade`.
- **Determinism:** every loop is in ascending id order; the same seed and commands give the same state (tested by comparing JSON). A JSON round-trip mid-game continues identically.

Refusal codes: `not_your_turn`, `cannot_afford`, `too_close`, `not_connected`, `no_pieces`, `offer_closed`, `table_closed`, plus `invalid_site` (the core code: occupied spot, not our settlement, settlement and road out of order in setup) and `bad_trade` (malformed trade or wrong discard count). `leader_refuses` is declared for G1-2 but not used.

Naming: the building commands are `PlaceTableSettlement`, `PlaceTableRoad`, `UpgradeTableCity` because Economy already has `PlaceRoad` and `UpgradeCity`. The others follow Appendix A (`Sacrifice`, `LeaveTable`, `BankTrade`, `OfferTrade`, `AnswerOffer`, `EndTurn`) plus `DiscardCards`.
