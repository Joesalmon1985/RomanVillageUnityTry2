# G1-6b: god board UI layout pass

Work on branch `godboard/g1-6b-ui-layout`. Follows `Docs/GODBOARD_UI.md` (layout patterns only; all art is our own, drawn in code).

## Done
1. People panels across the top in banner colours: emblem disc, name, large points badge, small counters (cards held, road length, soldiers), longest-road marker on its holder, active panel lifted and ringed, hourglass ring draining round the badge (full ring for AI turns).
2. Hand as a fan of five goods cards at the bottom centre with count badges; empty goods dimmed; cards lift when picked in trade or discard.
3. Costs card at the bottom left (road, camp, city as goods pictures); affordable rows lit; tapping a row enters that build mode (`GodBoardDirector.BuildMode`, `SetBuildMode`) and lights only that kind of legal spot; tapping again, building, or ending the turn clears it. A row you cannot afford says why in the status strip.
4. Spare-piece stacks (road, camp, city) beside the costs card; the "Roads left" text is gone.
5. One big round hourglass button (bottom right) ends the turn; the text button column is gone. A round trade button sits beside it.
6. Small round icon buttons along the top-right: return to the world, hint (lit when on), plague overlay (drawn by `GodBoardHud`, state via `PlagueIsOn`/`PlagueToggle` set by `PlagueHud`), zoom in, zoom out. The settings icon is not done (no settings screen on the board yet).
7. Trade modal: give row and get row of goods cards, Peoples / Bank or port switch, bank rates as pictures, people emblems (and All) to pick the recipient, round cross and tick. Discard picker in the same style with the timer bar in the title band. Offer cards above the hourglass with round tick/cross and the expiry as a draining edge.
8. Event feed is now a collapsible log tab at the bottom left (closed by default, unread badge); toasts (two at most) at the top centre; big moment cards: "The gods are angry." (epidemic), crystal free city (`SpireRaised`), a camp or city burned (`SettlementBurned`), the prince's advice (small card). `GodBoardDirector.Moment`, `ShowMoment`.
9. Port tokens in the top view past each jetty (`PortMarkers`): the good's picture for 2:1, a ring of the five goods for 3:1.
10. Collecting villagers (G1-7 open problem): each now has an overhead pin in the good's colour and a dotted trail on the ground from the camp to the hex.

No numbers or odds on the board (points, card counts, road length, soldiers are counts, not odds). `Assets/World/Game` still only reads state and sends commands.
