**The ruleset switch (G0-2).**

- `World.Sim.Ruleset { Legacy = 0, GodBoard = 1 }` (`Sim/Core/Ruleset.cs`; "BoardRules" in the PRD) and `WorldState.Ruleset` (saved; default Legacy). `GameSetup.Ruleset` and `GameSetup.TableHumanSeat` (0, or -1 for an AI-only table). `GameSetup.GodBoard` now sets `Ruleset.GodBoard`; `GameSetup.Legacy` and `CreateNew(seed)` are unchanged.

Kept on GodBoard: the logistics systems, factions state, `Advise`, the campaign and halt hooks, eras, ledger, campaign, council.

**Wiring (`Sim/GodBoard/GodBoardModule.cs`).**

- `InitState(s, humanSeat)`: `WorldState.Table` for the game's peoples on `Board.StartCorners` (seat 0 the player), ports from the board; `WorldState.Plague` initialised. Runs after every older module, so their random streams are unchanged.
- Ports (`PortsFromBoard`): each board port gives a `TablePort` with `Hex`, `Good` (-1 = 3:1) and `Corners` = the port's Meadow hex corners that are legal settlement corners (`PlotLayout.IsSettlementCorner`, three hexes meet), ascending; `CornerA`/`CornerB` are the first two. New `TablePort.Corners`/`Hex` fields; `TableRules.TradeRate` uses all corners (`TableRules.UsesPort`).
- Seams (new on `TableSeams`: `OnEpidemic`, `OnRoundEnded`, `OnAgeEnded`, called by `TableEngine`):
  - `HexBlocked` → `PlagueApi.HexBlocked` (TU-2, PL-9).
  - a 7, after discards, → `PlagueRules.Epidemic` (PL-6; does nothing in the grace rounds).
  - end of each round (after the last turn, before the next roll) → `PlagueRules.InfectRound` (PL-5).
  - `AgeEnded` → `PlagueRules.End` (HI-8) and `ErasLogic.MarkReady(ch.historic)` (raises `ChapterReady`). The step itself (`AdvanceChapter`, temples falling, board closing) is left to G1-8: the Game reads `ChapterReady`/`AgeEnded`.
- `Register(sim)` (called by `SaveCodec.CreateSim` for GodBoard states, so new and loaded games are wired the same): plague `Banish` handler, table engine and handlers, seams. `GodBoardModule.Seams(sim)` returns them. `GodBoardModule.WireAi(seams)` is the empty spot for G1-2's brain (until then the table's `TableBasicAi` plays).
- Table points are **not** fed to the Ledger: the ledger pays keys once and never takes points back, while table points drop when a settlement burns. Not trivial; left for whoever needs ledger points (CC-6 says points come only from the board, so readers should use `TableRules.Points`).

**Save.** `SaveCodec.CurrentVersion` = 6. `Ruleset`, `Table`, `Plague`, `Peoples`, `Board.Ports` are plain fields of the state and serialise as they are. Migration 5 → 6: Legacy, no table, no plague, peoples filled from players. Version 4 still loads (4 → 5 → 6).

## Findings and open problems

1. **The island almost always falls in AI-only play.** With nobody banishing (no player, no war yet), seeds 1-40 on the GodBoard AI-only game: the island fell (8 outbreaks) in 39 of 40, typically in rounds 16-50; only seed 21 stood. Blocked hexes also stretch games (seed 20261119: 28 rounds, 119 blocked hex productions; the 40 seeds ended in rounds 13 to 50). The war (soldiers purging, G3-2), the player's banish and crystal cures are the counterplay; until then plague numbers need tuning or the board proof will be dominated by doom.
2. **IslandFell does not stop the table.** PL-10 says the game ends for everyone; nothing ends the table or raises a game-over yet. The table keeps playing to 10 points with a fallen plague (the plague itself stops). Needs an owner of the "island falls" ending (G2-2 view, a sim game-over).
3. **Table settlements may sit on any corner**, including rim corners where fewer than three hexes meet (G1-1's `SettlementRefusal` checks only the distance rule). The world's camps and the ports use three-hex corners only. If the table keeps this, a settlement on an outer corner of a port hex gets no port; decide in G1-2 or a fix to G1-1.
4. The economy's start camps still get their `StartMature` hex stock at InitState on GodBoard; collectors harvest it once and then stand idle (there is no growth). Collecting villagers on a roll is G1-7.
5. `AgesApi.CampaignMayStart` (the handover gate, CC-1) is unchanged: G2-3.
6. PlayMode tests and Unity compilation not run (no Unity in this package).
