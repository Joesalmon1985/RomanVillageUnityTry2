# Strategy, Military and Save API

Milestone 4 (simulation). Code: `Assets/World/Sim/Strategy` (`World.Sim.Strategy`), `Sim/Military` (`World.Sim.Military`), `Sim/Save` (`World.Sim.Save`). All commands go through `WorldSim.Submit` with `PlayerId = PlayerIds.Human`; every refusal has a code and a sentence. Read state freely; never write it from views.

**Flow.** Prologue: rounds before the quartermaster commission (no draft, demons or rival; `FoundCity`/`BuildWonderStage` refuse `prologue`). Age I starts at the first round start after the commission (or at round `MaxPrologueRounds`+1 = 9). Then 3 ages x 2 rounds (`StrategyTuning.Ages`, `RoundsPerAge`). At each age start both sides are dealt a draft (pause the clock with `PauseReasons.Draft` while `StrategyApi.DraftPending`). `EndRound` (economy command) runs: rival turn (plan VP on age-rounds 2, 4, 6; garrison top-up; raids from Age II), infection draw, points check; after the last round the game ends by points (`age_end`). First to 8 VP at a tick boundary wins; 4 breaches loses.

**State.** `WorldState.Strategy` (`StrategyState`): `Started, FirstRound, Age, RoundInAge, GameOver, Outcome (None|Victory|Defeat|Draw), OutcomeReason (points|breach|age_end|outpost), EndedTick, Demons[vertex], Breaches, InfectionDeck (top = last)/InfectionDiscard/LastInfection, HeroVertex, HeroMovesLeft, Cards[id]{DefId, Age}, Decks[age-1], DraftStep (0 none, 1, 2), DraftAge, Sides[player]{Hand, DraftHand, Built[{CardId, CityVertex}], Discarded, PlanVp}, Wonder{BuildingId, StagesDone, InProgressStage}, Ledger, Tuning`. Cities live in `Economy.Cities` (the rival outpost is a rival city there).
`WorldState.Military` (`MilitaryState`): `Squads[id]{Owner, Kind (Field|Garrison|Raid), Alive, Units[{Id, Hp, Cooldown}], At, CurrentEdge, FromVertex, ToVertex, Progress, Order (None|Defend|Attack|Return), Target, Engaged, Occupying, BarracksId}`, `Queues[{BuildingId, Orders[{Supplied, Progress, Rations, Ingots}], HomeSquadId, Reason, ReasonWare}]`, `Outpost{Vertex, Hp, MaxHp, Destroyed, DestroyedTick, GarrisonSquadId}`, `Unpowered`, `UnitsTrained`, `ConsumedForUnits`, `Tuning`.

**Commands** (refusals besides `game_over` and `not_owner`, which every command can give):
| Command | Fields | Refusals |
|---|---|---|
| `FoundCity` | `Vertex` (adds `CarriersPerCity` = 2 carriers at the storehouse) | `prologue`, `bad_vertex`, `occupied`, `too_close` (any city or the outpost on a neighbouring flag), `no_road` (none of your roads touches the flag), `cannot_afford` (cost Planks 2, Stone 3, Grain 1; names the shortfall) |
| `Trade` | `Give, Get, Count` (Count received) | `same_ware`, `bad_count`, `cannot_afford`. Rate 4:1, -1 with an active Market, -1 per Trade Charter, min 2 |
| `Sell` / `Buy` | `Ware, Count` | `bad_count` (Sell in lots of `SellWaresPerCoin`, 2), `cannot_afford`. Buy costs 3 coin per ware |
| `MoveHero` | `Vertex` (neighbouring flag) | `bad_vertex`, `no_turn_action` (4 moves per round + Ranger Post), `not_adjacent`. Arrival clears all demons |
| `DraftPick` | `CardId` (from your draft hand) | `not_drafting`, `not_in_draft`. The rival picks at the same tick; after pick 1 hands swap |
| `BuildCard` | `CardId, CityVertex` | `not_in_hand`, `no_city`, `city_full` (3 per city), `cannot_afford` |
| `DiscardCard` | `CardId` | `not_in_hand` (pays 3 coin) |
| `BuildWonderStage` | - | `prologue`, `wonder_complete`, `stage_in_progress`, `no_wonder` (needs an active Wonder site). Requests the stage wares to the site; completes when they are delivered (stage 1 Planks 4 + Stone 6 = 1 VP; stage 2 Rations 2 + Ingots 2 = 2 VP) |
| `QueueUnit` | `BarracksId, Count` | `no_barracks`, `bad_count`, `queue_full` (8). Each unit requests 1 rations + 1 ingots to the barracks |
| `OrderSquad` | `SquadId, Order (Defend|Attack), Target` | `unknown_squad`, `bad_order`, `bad_vertex` |
| `CancelOrder` | `SquadId` | `unknown_squad`, `no_order` (stops at the next flag) |
`EndRound` additionally refuses `draft_pending` and `game_over` (economy hook `EndRoundGuards`).

**Events.** Strategy: `AgeStarted{Age, Round}`, `StrategyRoundStarted{Age, RoundInAge, HeroMoves}`, `CityFounded{PlayerId, Vertex, CityId}`, `Traded`, `Sold`, `Bought`, `DemonSpawned{Vertex, Count}`, `DemonRepelled{Vertex}`, `BreachAdvanced{Vertex, Breaches, Limit}`, `DemonsCleared{Vertex, Count, By: hero|squad}`, `InfectionDrawn{Vertices}`, `HeroMoved{From, To, MovesLeft}`, `DraftDealt{Age, PlayerCards}`, `DraftPicked{PlayerId, CardId, DefId, Step}`, `DraftPassed{PlayerCards}`, `DraftFinished{Age, DiscardedCards}`, `CardBuilt{PlayerId, CardId, DefId, CityVertex}`, `CardDiscarded{..., Coin}`, `WonderStageOrdered{Stage, BuildingId}`, `WonderStageCompleted{Stage, Vp}`, `RivalScored{Vp, Total}`, `GameEnded{Outcome, Reason, PlayerVp, RivalVp}`.
Military: `UnitQueued`, `TrainingStarted{BarracksId}`, `UnitTrained{BarracksId, SquadId, UnitId}`, `QueueStateChanged{BarracksId, Reason, ReasonWare}`, `PowerChanged{BuildingId, Powered}`, `SquadOrdered`, `SquadMoved{SquadId, From, To}` (animate over `MilitaryTuning.TicksPerEdge` = 40), `SquadArrived`, `SquadEngaged{Vertex, SquadIds}`, `UnitHit{Vertex, AttackerUnitId, TargetUnitId (-1 = outpost), Damage, TargetHpLeft}`, `UnitDied`, `SquadDestroyed`, `OutpostDamaged{Hp, MaxHp}`, `OutpostDestroyed`, `GarrisonReinforced{Guards}`, `RaidLaunched{SquadId, Target, Units}`, `RaidOccupied{SquadId, Vertex}`, `RaidEnded{SquadId, How: leaving|merged|disbanded}`.

**Query helpers for panels** (`StrategyApi`, all `(WorldState s, ...)`):
- HUD: `Status(s)` -> `StrategyStatus{Started, Age, Ages, RoundInAge, RoundsPerAge, RoundsLeft, DraftPending, GameOver, Outcome, OutcomeReason, Headline, Breaches, BreachLimit, HeroVertex, HeroMovesLeft, NextInfection, Coin}`; `Headline(s)` ("Age II, round 1 of 2.", "Victory: 8 points to 5 (first to 8 points)."); `Roman(n)`; `AgeRound(s)`.
- Score: `Score(s, player)` -> `ScoreBreakdown{Cities, Cards, Wonder, Outpost, Plan, Total}`; `ScoreText(s, player)` ("Cities 4, cards 1, wonder 1, outpost 0: 6 of 8.").
- Cities: `CheckCity(s, v)` (same as the command), `CitySites(s)` (every flag with Legal/Reason/Message), `LegalCitySites(s, ignoreCost)`, `CityCostText(s)`, `PlayerCities(s)`.
- Market: `TradeRate(s)`, `SellWaresPerCoin(s)`, `BuyPrice(s)`, `MarketText(s)`.
- Demons and hero: `Demons(s)` (vertex, count), `DemonsAt(s, v)`, `BreachThreshold(s)`, `InfectionCount(s)`, `HeroMoveTargets(s)`.
- Cards: `DraftHand(s)`, `Hand(s)`, `BuiltCards(s, player)`, `CardsAtCity(s, player, v)`, `CitiesWithCardRoom(s)`, `DescribeCard(s, id)` -> `CardView{CardId, DefId, Name, Age, CostText, EffectText, CityVertex, Affordable}`, `CardDefOf(s, id)`, `Effects(s, player)`; table `CardTable.All` / `Get(id)` / `ForAge(age)`.
- Wonder: `Wonder(s)` -> `WonderInfo{StagesDone, StageCount, InProgressStage, BuildingId, Status ("Wonder stage 1 is waiting for 2 planks, 6 stone."), Lines ("planks 2/4"), CanOrder}`.
- `WaresSpent(s)` (strategy ledger per ware).

`MilitaryApi`: `Power(s)` -> `PowerSummary{Supply, Demand, FuelledPlants, Consumers[{BuildingId, Type, Demand, Powered}] (allocation order), Sentence ("Power 0 for 2 needed: the Barracks has no power.")}`, `IsPowered(s, id)`; `DescribeBarracks(s, id)` -> `BarracksInfo{Queued, HeadSupplied, ProgressPercent, TrainingTicks, Reason, WaitingFor ("1 rations"), Status ("The barracks is waiting for 1 rations and 1 ingots."), CostText}`; `TrainingTicks(s)`, `UnitCostText(s)`; `Squads(s, player = -1)` / `DescribeSquad(s, id)` -> `SquadView{Id, Owner, Kind, Units, TotalHp, MaxHp, At, From, To, ProgressPermille, Moving, Engaged, Occupying, Order, Target, OrderText}`; `Outpost(s)` -> `OutpostInfo{Vertex, Hp, MaxHp, Destroyed, Guards, Status}`; `RaidedFlags(s)`.

**Rules in brief.** Infection: InfectionBase 2 + 1 per age draws (Beacon Tower -1, min 1) from a shuffled deck of the flags within 2 edges of home (home added in Age II; discard reshuffled when empty). A drawn flag gains a demon; one already holding 3 (4 with Ward Stones) adds a breach; a demon on the hero's flag is cleared at once. Intensify (default on): from Age II the discard is shuffled back on top before each draw, so flags left with demons are drawn again and breach. Demon or raid flags block carriers (`demon_on_route`); a demon on a city flag stops that city's batches. Batches at your cities grow by 2 per age (`AgeBatchBonus`) plus card bonuses. Power: 4 per fuelled power plant; demand 2 per enabled refinery and per barracks with a queue; consumers sorted by building priority (high first) then id; the last ones lose power (`no_power`). Training (150 ticks; Drill Yard -40%) starts only when the unit's wares are in the barracks store and advances only while powered; units form one squad per barracks (max 4). Squads walk any edges; combat on fixed ticks (HP 10, damage 3, cooldown 10; guards HP 8, damage 2), simultaneous, each attacker hits the first living enemy (squad id, then unit order); with no guards left, units hit the outpost (60). Outpost destroyed: +2 VP, rival plan VP and raids stop. Garrison 2 + 1 per age (returning raiders only refill it to that size). Raids (2 guards) walk to the player road flag nearest the outpost (not the storehouse), hold it for a round, then go home.

**Save** (`World.Sim.Save.SaveCodec`): `string ToJson(WorldState, PlayerPose pose = null, IDictionary<string,string> extras = null)`; `SaveModel FromJson(string)` (`Version`, `Tick`, `Player{X, Y, Z, Yaw, Pitch}`, `Extras` sorted string map, `State`; throws `FormatException` for empty, broken or newer saves); `WorldSim CreateSim(WorldState)` registers Economy, Strategy and Military (systems, handlers, hooks) without InitState; `WorldSim Load(json, out SaveModel)`; `StateJson(state)` for comparisons. Save between ticks: queued commands are not saved. Ward progress is inside `WorldState.Ward`.

**Edits outside these folders:** `Economy/EconomyHooks.EndRoundGuards` (list of `Func<WorldState, CommandResult>`) checked by `EndRound`; `EconomyApi.AddCarrier(s)` (new idle carrier at the storehouse).

**Balance (scripted, `StrategyPlaythroughTests`):** competent player reaches 8 VP in about 7-12 min on the default seed and 10 others; full player (barracks, squad of 4, attack) ends in 12-15 min; ignoring demons loses by breach.
