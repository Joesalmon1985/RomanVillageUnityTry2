**How the runs are made.** Every run starts from `dev.historic` for its seed (the faction game really runs underneath: the prince is autonomous and builds alone, the Ash Clan plays, demons are on, overruns are the campaign setback) and sends `StartCampaign` with a shipped pack: `authored_v1` (Partial: the Earth route of Slice 1) or `skeleton_v1` (Complete: four trivial routes, the finale and both endings). Scripted players only send campaign commands and read `CampaignApi`; nobody casts a spell for the player's people. Duel results are injected through `ResolveEncounter` unless a section says they were played: then a real `WardDuelSession` was played on the attempt's own seed against the encounter's opponent profile by the scripted first-timer (`WardBalanceTests.FirstTimer`) or competent player of `WardOpponentTests`. Time is game time since the campaign started (m:ss; 10 ticks = 1 s). A pending duel is resolved on the next tick, as the game does with its world clock paused during the duel. Seeds: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5.

## I. Conservation and idempotence

Every run of this report (101 runs, 394,401 ticks) was checked on every tick by the driver, and none broke a rule:

- **Custody in exactly one state.** Each of the four crystals is always in one of the five custody states, at the place that state means (on its site while AtSite or RivalTransit, on its plan step's altar while AtRivalAltar, at its tower when Restored), and only changes through a `CrystalCustodyChanged` whose `From` is the state it was in. Nothing leaves Restored; a carried crystal only becomes Restored (never lost, even in the retreats and overruns of these runs). Four crystals in every state: none lost.
- **Attempts resolved once.** No attempt id is recorded or resolved twice; every second `ResolveEncounter` sent in sections C and D was refused with `already_resolved`.
- **Points.** The player's points always equal the crystals restored, the Ash Clan's the crystals ever set on an altar (paid once even when a crystal goes back to an altar); no run unlocked the Modern roster (the ledger holds only crystal keys in H1: at most 4 each, below the 10-point threshold).
- **The dead never speak.** Every 600 ticks (639 checks) every line whose speaker is a fallen companion was asked for through `CampaignApi.Line` and came back empty; the faction game's own invariants (`SimTestKit.CheckInvariants`: wares, sites, carriers) held at the same moments.
- **Essential clues with zero companions.** All six were killed on the first tick, and the companions' own clues were taken out of the run's copy of the pack (a companion's clue is given on the opening tick, before any command can kill them; this is the state of a party that lost everyone before learning anything): the authored Earth route still reaches the glade through the sawyer (Odo's shortcut gone) and the Deepstone is restored at 1:53; the stolen Deepstone is recovered with nobody to hold the way out (the keeper duel is fought with no volunteer and won) and restored at 1:40; the skeleton reaches its fire site through the elder (Petra's shortcut gone) and ends `restored` at 6:50.

## Known weaknesses

- **The authored pack has one route.** Sections A to C and F run Earth only; the full campaign (H, D, G) runs on the skeleton, whose routes are a few steps long. Length and race numbers for the real campaign wait for the Slice 2 routes.
- **The race is not a race yet** (section H): with the PRD's 8-minute first march nobody who follows Mira loses the Deepstone. Not retuned here: it is an owner number.
- **Overruns.** The faction game ran with its default tuning and 64 overruns happened naturally across all runs (H counts them per run; each of your camps lost charged the expedition +1800 on that tick, and no run ended by overrun). But none landed in a moment this report examines closely (mid-route with the party underground, or at a threshold); that case is proven in `CampaignSeamTests` with a forced warning, the overrun itself raised by the real `StrengthSystem`.
- **Doubled journal lines.** On taking and restoring a crystal the journal gets the runtime's own sentence and the pack's journal line for the same moment (section A, 0:39 and 1:46). Harmless but untidy: the runtime (I1) or the pack (I2) should drop one.
- **The validator asks a friendly encounter for a threshold, safe point and stage** (section E had to give `orrin_demo` all three), while `CAMPAIGN_FORMAT.md` says only lethal ones need a stage. Reported to the validator's owner (I2, G2); not changed here.
- **The faction game does not touch the Earth route's timing** unless an overrun lands: A and the prompt rows of H are identical across seeds. The seeds vary the island's economy and the overruns, not the campaign.
- **Duels.** Injected results stand in for the duel where a consequence is the point; real duels use the scripted Ward players of the Ward tests, not people. The world clock is not stepped during a duel here, as the game pauses it; the proof does not check the game's pause itself.
- **The "Ash got there first" start** (F2, I) uses the developer command `DevRivalNextStep`; section B reaches the same state with ordinary commands ("Not that way").
- **Friendly duels** are checked on an encounter added to a copy of the skeleton, because Orrin's demonstration is Slice 2 data.
- **Scouting** happens only when a belief goes stale while the player is away; on these short routes Tansy is sent rarely (F2 says how often). Scout clues and the stale "where to look" sentence are covered by the runtime tests, not here.
- **The dead never speak** is checked through `CampaignApi.Line` (the only way lines reach the game) every 600 ticks and at each death's next check, not on every tick.
- **Determinism** is checked by building this whole report twice in one process; a run on another machine or runtime is not compared.
