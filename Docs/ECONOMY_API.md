# Simulation API (prehistoric village)

The pure C# simulation of `Docs/REFACTOR_SPEC.md` as built on branch `refactor/sim` (13 Nov 2026). Code: `Assets/World/Sim` (assembly `World.Sim`). The binding names are appendix A of the spec (pinned by `ApiSurfaceTests`); everything added since is in its change log (A.5). The rules are spec sections 2 to 6; this file says how the code behaves where the spec leaves room, and what the game layer can call. Proof that it works: `Docs/ECONOMY_PROOF.md` (generated by the test `ProofReport`).

The game layer never writes state: it reads `WorldState` and the read helpers below, submits commands through `WorldSim.Submit`, and animates events drained by `GameClock`.

## Modules and tick order

`WorldSimFactory.CreateNew(seed)` builds the board and two `PlayerState`s ("Your people", "the Ash Clan"), then `EconomyModule`, `FactionsModule`, `DemonsModule` (`InitState`, then `Register`). `SaveCodec.CreateSim(state)` registers the same three on a loaded state (save version 4; version 3 is refused with "The save is from an older version (3) with a different world.").

Systems per tick, after the queued commands: Dice 100, Growth 150, Collect 200, Production 300 (workshops, lodges, meals), Construction 400, Jobs 500 (demand, carrier assignment), Carriers 600 (movement, pick-up, delivery), Brain 1150 (planning, the rival's casts), Strength 1200 (strength, warning, endings). Demons react to rolls through `EconomyHooks.OnRolled` (no system of their own).

## Board and plots

- `Board`: `Radius` 4, `HexTerrain[61]`, `HexNumber[61]` (2..12, never 7; 0 Meadow), `StartCorners` = [70, 97] on every seed, `SuggestedSettlementCorners`, `ReservedPlots` = [635] (the dance circle). Numbers per spec 2.A.1: start hexes Forest 6, Hills 8, Fields 5; Mountains a seeded order of {5, 6, 9, 10}; the other 28 a seeded shuffle of the pool.
- `PlotLayout` (pure): plot id = corner * 9 + sector * 3 + slot (sector = index of the hex in the corner's adjacent hexes, ascending). Slots 0 and 1 at 30 m along the spoke and 5 * sqrt(3) m to the left / right; slot 2 at 45 m on the spoke (cities only). Every corner, hex centre and plot centre is a cell of the 5 m walking lattice (tested). `PathPoints(t, plot)` is the local path from the corner; `LocalSteps` 8 or 9. `IsValidPlot`, `PlotCount`, `Yaw(dx, dz)` are helpers.
- `EconomyApi.Plot(s, plot)` gives a `PlotView` with `Use`: `NoSettlement` (no finished camp owns the corner), `Reserved`, `Locked` (slot 2 of a camp that is not a city), `Free`, `Site` (site or constructing), `Building` (active or retiring). `PlotsOf(s, settlementId)` lists the usable plots (6, a city 9, the home city 8).

## Wares and buildings

Ten wares (`World.Sim.Ware`): Wood 0, Clay 1, Grain 2, Sheep 3, Stone 4 (raw), Planks 5, Bricks 6, Bread 7, Meat 8, CutStone 9 (worked). `Wares.Title(CutStone)` = "Cut stone". `EconomyApi.WareColour` gives "#RRGGBB" per ware.

Sixteen building types (`BuildingDefs.All`, `Get(type)`, `PrimitiveFor(terrain)`, `ImprovedFor(terrain)`), recipes, costs and build times as spec 3.2 and 3.3; every `BuildingDef.Purpose` is one sentence. Primitive collectors come with a camp (slot 0 of each resource sector) and are not placeable. An improved collector, when finished, sets the primitive collector of its hex in that camp to `Retiring`: it finishes its cycle, its store is carried away, then it is `Retired` (`BuildingRetired`) and the plot is free.

## Commands and refusals

| Command | Checked by | Applied |
|---|---|---|
| `CastSpell { ProposalId }` | the proposal is open and the people's own; then its placement check; failure: `no_longer_legal` with "That can no longer be done: " + the underlying sentence | places the site or banishes; `SpellCast`, `ProposalRemoved("cast")` |
| `DeclineProposal { ProposalId }` | open and own | `ProposalRemoved("declined")`; the same `Key` is not proposed for `DeclineCooldownTicks` |
| `PlaceBuilding { Type, Plot }` | `EconomyApi.CheckBuilding` | building site; `SitePlaced` |
| `PlaceRoad { HexEdge }` | `CheckRoad` | road site, source settlement chosen by rule |
| `FoundSettlement { Corner }` | `CheckSettlement` | settlement site, source settlement chosen by rule |
| `UpgradeCity { Corner }` | `CheckCity` | city upgrade site (id = settlement id) |
| `CancelSite { Kind, Id }` | an unfinished site of yours | delivered goods back to the source storehouse; jobs released; goods on carriers brought home; state `Cancelled` |
| `Banish { SiteKind, SiteId }` | demons there and the site is yours | all demons removed; `DemonsBanished` |

Refusal codes and exact sentences: spec 5.2 (`EconomyRefusals`, `FactionRefusals`, `DemonRefusals`, each with `Sentence(...)`). Check order for a building: game over, people, type, placeable, plot, camp (none / another people's / not finished), reserved, locked, taken, terrain, already built. A hex edge id out of range is refused with `not_connected`. `PlayerId` is the people (0 player, 1 rival); the sim does not refuse direct build commands in normal play (the game simply does not expose them).

## Dice and hex stock

A deck of the 36 ordered outcomes (`DiceState.Deck`, encoded d1 * 10 + d2, top = last), reshuffled with its own `Rng` when empty. One roll every `RollIntervalTicks` from `FirstRollTick`. A roll of N (not 7) adds `BatchSize` young units of the terrain's ware to every hex numbered N with at least one adjacent finished camp that is not demon-held, up to `HexCap` (the rest is `StockLost`). Cohorts mature `GrowTicks[terrain]` after they were added (`StockMatured`). `EconomyApi.HexStock` gives the three young stages (first, second, last third of growth), `NextMatureTick`, `YoungVisible` (false for Hills and Mountain) and `Stopped`.

Collectors (ascending building id, so the lower id wins a tie) take 1 mature unit at the start of a cycle and put `Yield` wares in their store at the end; a store with no room for another yield waits (`output_full`); no mature stock: `no_batch` ("Log pile is waiting for forest stock to grow."); a demon-held camp's collectors start nothing.

## Logistics (spec 2.B.1)

- Each camp's storehouse is its hub. Demand jobs are created every tick in serving order: construction (building sites from the own storehouse; road and settlement sites from their source camp, up to `ShipmentSize` of one ware per job), workshop and lodge inputs (`InputBuffer` cycles on hand or on the way), outputs of collectors and workshops to the storehouse (one unit per job), then imports.
- Import: when a camp's still-unjobbed demand of a ware exceeds its unreserved stock and no shipment of that ware is heading there, the nearest connected camp of the same people (by road route, ties lower corner) whose unreserved stock exceeds its own unjobbed demand ships `min(ShipmentSize, need, spare)`.
- A job reserves its goods at the source (`Reserved`) and counts at the destination (`Incoming`) until delivered. Carriers belong to their camp (5 at a start camp, 3 at a new one, +1 per finished placed building, +1 for a city).
- Assignment: waiting jobs in serving order (sites, inputs, outputs, shipments), ties by job id; a job waiting `JobAgeTicks` or more goes first (so nothing starves). A carrier at its storehouse picks up there at once; for an output it first walks to the building.
- Legs: local legs follow the plot path (`LocalSteps * CarrierTicksPerStep`); main-road legs follow `Carrier.LegCorners` (12 steps per hex edge, 6 more to a road site's midpoint). After a delivery to a building the carrier takes back a waiting output of that building, else it walks home (`Returning`). After a shipment or a road or camp site delivery it walks the same corners back.
- Routes (`Logistics.Route`): the people's built roads only, never through another people's camp, avoiding demon-held edges and corners. A carrier on a main-road leg waits while the edge it is on or entering is demon-held, and walks on after the banish. A waiting job that has lost its route is released (`JobStalled`) and demand re-creates it from a reachable source. Demon-held camps send and receive no shipments and no road or camp site goods; their local trips go on.
- A city upgrade moves goods straight from its own storehouse to `UpgradeDelivered` each tick (no carriers), as the spec says; a shortage there is imported like any other demand.

## Positions and descriptions

- `EconomyApi.CarrierPosition(s, id, tickFraction, out x, out z, out yaw)`: position along the current leg at `(Progress + tickFraction) / LegTicks` of its path length (plot path, or corner path plus road-site midpoint); yaw of the current segment. Idle carriers stand on their storehouse corner. False for an unknown id.
- `DescribeCarrier` ("A carrier of the home camp: carrying 1 wood from the log pile to the storehouse of the home camp."), `DescribeJob`, `DescribeBuilding` (title, status sentence, recipe, cost, store lines), `WaitingSentence(kind, id)` (null when progressing), `SettlementName` ("the home camp", "the Ash Clan's home camp", "the camp by the pasture and hills"), `CostText` ("2 wood + 1 clay").

## Warriors, strength and endings

A Warrior lodge (spec: once the people has a city; tuned: also once it has an active Bakery and Smokehouse) trains a warrior per cycle from 1 Bread + 1 Meat on hand, up to 3 per lodge. Each warrior eats 1 Bread (else Meat) every `MealTicks` straight from its camp's storehouse; a miss makes it hungry (counts 0, retries every `MealRetryTicks`). Strength = camps 1, cities 2, fed warriors 1, recomputed every tick (`StrengthChanged`, `Faction.StrengthChangedTick`); with `DemonHeldCampsCount` false (the game) a camp or city with demons on its corner adds nothing (`Faction.HeldCamps`). Overrun warning, cancel, overrun and the time limit as spec 2.C.3, but no warning starts before `WarningGraceTicks`. `GameOutcome` is from the player's side. After the end no planning happens and every command is refused with `game_over`; the dice and the economy keep running.

`FactionApi`: `Proposals`, `Top`, `GetProposal`, `ProposalTitle`, `ProposalTarget` (point and press radius: plot 5, corner 6, road midpoint 30 with a 6 m strip), `ProposalAt` (tests the strip for roads and road banishes), `CastRangeMetres`, `Strength`, `Warning` (with a sentence and `TicksLeft`), `Outcome` (with a sentence), `Headline`.

## The prince's brain

Every `PlanIntervalTicks` (player at ticks where `Tick % 20 == 0`, from tick 0; rival at `% 20 == 10`) the brain builds candidates that are legal and affordable now (covered by the unreserved stock of the camps connected to the target's camp), drops declined keys, sorts by score then `Key`, keeps 3, keeps the ids of unchanged keys, and fires `ProposalAdded`, `ProposalRemoved("expired")`, `ProposalsReordered` (a new top gets `TopSinceTick` = now). It plans nothing but banishes while the people has `MaxOpenSites` unfinished sites. The rival casts its top proposal (an illegal top is removed as expired); it never declines. Spec rule: once the proposal has been top for `RivalCastDelayTicks`. Game rule (`RivalPacePerCast`): once `FactionApi.RivalCastDelay(s)` has passed since its last cast and the top has been top for `RivalSettleTicks`; the delay is `RivalCastDelayTicks`, less `RivalBoldTicksPerLead` per point of strength the rival leads by, never under `RivalMinDelayTicks` (optionally ramping over time with `RivalCastDelayEndTicks` / `RivalRampTicks`, unused).

Candidates and scores (spec 6.2 with the tuning above): opening (Woodcutter's hut on Forest slot 1 of the start camp, 95, until cast); banish 100 + 10 per demon on a camp, 40 + 10 per demon on a road edge (100 + 10 when a carrier waits on it or a road or camp site of the people is held up by demons); improved collectors 40 + 2 x pips (+10 for the scarcest raw ware of the next road and camp, or of the inputs of the people's workshops; +15 once the people has 4 camps); next road towards the expansion target 50 + value / 2 (merchant); a camp at the target when a road reaches it 60 + value / 2; workshops 45 / 44 / 42 / 41 / 40 (Sawmill, Kiln, Stonecutter, Bakery, Smokehouse) once the people collects their first input, + 30 once it has 2 camps, + 1 per unit of that input above 4 (up to 15), one of each type per people; city 90 (the camp with the most buildings); lodge 90 once a city exists or a Bakery and a Smokehouse are active (a second only when the first holds 3 warriors). Every candidate within 60 m of the people's last cast target gets +8 (walkable pitches; `Faction.LastCastX/Z`). Under a warning, the defender's camp, city and lodge candidates get +50 and a "gathering" sentence. Expansion target: the best free legal camp corner within 3 hex edges of the network over free edges, value = pips + 6 per raw ware not yet collected + 3 per distinct terrain - 4 per edge still needed, ties lower corner. Speakers: sawyer for the Woodcutter's hut after the opening and for the Sawmill, merchant for roads, prince for the rest.

## Demons

Sites: finished camp corners and built road edges of both peoples. After `GraceTicks`, every `SevensPerDraw`-th seven draws one site (spec: uniformly among all sites, corners ascending, then edges; with `DrawPerPeople`, the game: first a people with equal chances, then one of its sites uniformly) with the demons' own `Rng` and adds `PerSeven` demons; a site at `Cap` outbreaks instead (each site at most once per draw, chains allowed) to its built road edges (corner) or its camp ends and the built roads sharing an end (edge). `DemonApi.DemonSites` (with position, people and effect clause), `DemonsAt`, `SiteName`.

## Hooks (`EconomyApi.Hooks(sim)`)

`IsCornerBlocked`, `IsEdgeBlocked`, `IsSettlementHalted` (lists of `Func<WorldState, int, bool>`), `OnRolled` (`Action<WorldState, EventBuffer, int d1, int d2>`), `OnSiteCompleted` (`Action<WorldState, EventBuffer, SiteKind, int id>`). The Demons module fills the first four in its `Register`, so they are back after a load.

## Accounting (spec 4.4)

`WareLedger`: per ware `Starting`, `Collected`, `Produced`, `Consumed` (construction at completion, recipe inputs at a cycle's start, training, meals) and the sinks `RecipeIn/RecipeOut[type][ware]`, `Construction`, `Training`, `Meals`; hex stock `StockAdded` (starting mature stock included), `StockLost`, `StockTaken`, and `TakenByType[type]`. The tests check every 100 ticks: wares conserved, hex stock conserved, collected = taken x yield (less running cycles), reservations and incoming equal the open jobs, no idle carrier holds goods (`SimTestKit.CheckInvariants`).
